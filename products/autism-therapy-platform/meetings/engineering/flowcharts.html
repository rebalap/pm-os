J9 — DPDPA Consent Management

Two paths: Path A (consent capture at enrollment — prerequisite for all child health data) and Path B (data subject rights — view, correct, or request erasure). The erasure path has a legal conflict between DPDPA right to erasure and RPWD Act 2016 record retention obligations. Engineering must NOT auto-erase.

DPDPA 2023 applies (NOT HIPAA) Blocks ALL child health data until confirmed Erasure requires legal sign-off Two paths: capture and rights
