DPDPA 2023 compliance: Consent must be write-once and immutable. Pre-checked boxes are not permitted. If parent is not present, flag as staff-assisted verbal consent and require digital confirmation at next visit. No child health data may be entered until consent_status = confirmed.
H-06 (High risk): We believe Indian centers currently have no DPDPA-compliant consent flow. Paper consent forms do not reference digital data processing. This must be validated in primary fieldwork before launch.
