# Raw Notes 22 April Meeting

The summary of the meeting 

8 journeys were discussed for the platform lifecycle for a therapy center 

J1. Child enrolment or payment intake 
J2. Program design for therapy - in person programs for therapists 
J3. Session Notes or Clinical Notes 
J4. Progress reports 
J5 . Billing 
J6. Scheduling  or Attendance Management
J7. Dropout prevention or Patient engagement 
J8. Dashboard ana analytics 

The in-session data collection wasn’t even included for the scope since that is not something which is reimbursable. This can be a great value add for US and other markets where reimbursement is key. 

After walking through the overall journey, the engineering team  working on the EMR product suggested that all capabilities except  J2 and J4 for in person therapy. 

The focus of the MVP should be to reuse existing components as much as possible. This means the platform will act more as a clinical management tool rather than a therapy specific platform. 

Next Steps: 
Engineering will be analysing a bit more on the capabilities 
Product will do some primary research to validate and understand pinpoints of therapy centres before confirming features that are high risk to omit 
Product will define updated journeys and flows based on the discussion today. 

J2, J4, and insession data collection will be out of scope for MVP
