## Feature Brief: UPI-Native Billing

**Inspired by:** PractiPal (India), Practo (India)
**Prevalence:** Table stakes — Indian market
**Target user:** Rahul (Center Director / primary billing operator); Meena (parent, payer)
**What it does:** Rahul selects a family from the billing screen, confirms the amount due, and generates a UPI collect request. The platform sends the payment link to Meena via WhatsApp or SMS in a single tap. When Meena pays via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM, the platform auto-reconciles the payment against the child's fee record and marks the invoice as settled. No manual payment entry required.
**What "done" looks like:** Rahul can initiate and track a UPI payment request without leaving the platform; confirmed UPI payments auto-update the outstanding balance; Meena receives and completes the payment from WhatsApp without needing a separate app login.

**[ASSUMPTION — NOT VALIDATED]** This feature is assumed to solve the fee collection friction for Rahul (avoiding manual WhatsApp-based payment chasing) and reduce outstanding receivables. No primary research has confirmed that Rahul's UPI-collection workflow is sufficiently painful to drive adoption of a structured tool, or that Meena will complete a payment link rather than paying cash. Validate before committing engineering capacity.

## Feature Brief: Invoicing & Billing

**Inspired by:** TherapEZ (India), Jane App (Canada), SimplePractice (US), PractiPal (India)
**Prevalence:** Table stakes globally; **session-attendance auto-pull is a differentiator in the Indian market** (PractiPal does not do this)
**Target user:** Rahul (Center Director — generates and reviews invoices); Meena (parent — receives invoice)
**What it does:** At the end of each billing cycle, the platform auto-generates an invoice per family based on confirmed attendance records. Rahul configures each child's fee structure (per session / monthly flat / session package) once; the billing engine applies the rule automatically. Rahul reviews the draft invoice, adjusts if needed, and sends it. The platform tracks outstanding balances, triggers overdue alerts, and generates a receipt on payment confirmation.
**What "done" looks like:** Rahul can generate, review, and send all monthly invoices in under 10 minutes; the outstanding balance dashboard shows at a glance who owes what; receipts are auto-sent on payment without manual action.

**[ASSUMPTION — NOT VALIDATED]** This feature is assumed to solve Rahul's manual invoicing burden (currently Excel + WhatsApp) and reduce the awkwardness of fee conversations by making the process systematic and impersonal. No primary research has confirmed the actual time Rahul spends on billing admin or whether a structured invoicing tool would meaningfully improve collection rates. Validate before committing engineering capacity.

## Feature Brief: e-Prescriptions

**Inspired by:** Practo (India — clinic prescription workflow), Apollo 24/7 (India — digital prescription)
**Prevalence:** Table stakes in Indian telemedicine; **differentiator in autism therapy center SaaS** (no Indian therapy center tool has this)
**Target user:** Dr. Sunita (Clinical Supervisor — author); Meena (parent — recipient); external doctors (secondary recipient)
**What it does:** Dr. Sunita generates a structured digital prescription or referral letter for a child directly in the platform. The document includes her RCI license number, the child's details, structured fields for therapy recommendations, medication notes (if any, non-Schedule-H), dosage, follow-up instructions, and the center's letterhead. The completed document is shareable as a PDF with Meena via WhatsApp or secure link, and can be copied to an external paediatrician or doctor.
**What "done" looks like:** Dr. Sunita can generate a professionally formatted, printable e-prescription or referral letter in under 2 minutes; Meena receives it via WhatsApp; the document is stored against the child's record and retrievable at any time.

**[ASSUMPTION — NOT VALIDATED]** This feature is assumed to reduce the time Dr. Sunita spends drafting referral letters and prescription notes in Word/paper, and to improve the quality and legibility of documents shared with parents and external doctors. No primary research has confirmed that Dr. Sunita generates referral/prescription documents frequently enough for this to be a material time-saver, or that Meena values a structured digital document over a verbal recommendation. Validate before committing engineering capacity.

## Story UPI-001: Generate UPI payment request for a family

**As a** Rahul (Center Director)
**I want to** select a child's fee record and generate a UPI payment request for the outstanding amount
**So that** I have a shareable payment link tied to that specific family's balance without manually constructing a UPI request or sending my personal QR code

**Inspired by:** PractiPal UPI payment link generation

**Context:** End of billing cycle or whenever a family's balance shows as outstanding. Rahul is at his desk or on his Android phone. Child's invoice has already been generated (depends on INV-001 or manually entered amount). Outstanding balance is visible on the billing dashboard.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] AC-01: Given Rahul is on a child's billing record with a non-zero outstanding balance, when he taps "Request Payment", then the system displays a payment request screen pre-populated with the outstanding amount, child name, and center UPI VPA (Virtual Payment Address)
- [ ] AC-02: Given Rahul is on the payment request screen, when he adjusts the amount (partial payment scenario), then the system accepts any value between ₹1 and the full outstanding balance and validates it before proceeding
- [ ] AC-03: Given a valid payment request, when Rahul taps "Generate Link", then the system creates a unique UPI deep link (format: `upi://pay?pa=...`) and a short URL (for WhatsApp sharing) within 3 seconds
- [ ] AC-04: Given the payment link is generated, when Rahul taps "Send via WhatsApp", then the WhatsApp intent opens with a pre-filled message: "Dear [Parent Name], please find the payment link for [Child Name]'s therapy fees for [Month]: [link]. Total: ₹[amount]. — [Center Name]"
- [ ] AC-05: Given a payment link is outstanding, the system displays its status as "Pending" on the family's billing record until payment is confirmed or the link expires (default expiry: 48 hours, configurable)

**Edge Cases & Error States:**
- [ ] EC-01: If the center's UPI VPA is not configured, the system blocks link generation and prompts Rahul to complete UPI setup in Settings before proceeding
- [ ] EC-02: If the outstanding balance is ₹0, the "Request Payment" button is disabled with a tooltip: "No outstanding balance for this family"
- [ ] EC-03: If WhatsApp is not installed on the device, the system falls back to a native share sheet so Rahul can send the link via SMS, email, or any other app
- [ ] EC-04: If the UPI deep link generation fails (API timeout), the system displays "Could not generate payment link. Please try again." and logs the error for support visibility; Rahul's billing data is not affected

**Non-Functional Requirements:**
- Performance: Payment link generated within 3 seconds on a 4G connection; graceful failure message within 5 seconds if API is unavailable
- Offline: The "Request Payment" action requires network connectivity; if offline, surface "You're offline — payment links require an internet connection" and allow Rahul to queue the action for when connectivity is restored
- Accessibility: Touch targets ≥ 44px; "Request Payment" and "Generate Link" buttons must meet WCAG AA color contrast
- Privacy: ⚠️ DPDPA — child name and fee amount transmitted only over HTTPS; UPI reference stored in encrypted database field; center UPI VPA stored in server-side settings, not in client-side state

**Dependencies:**
- Blocked by: UPI VPA configuration story (UPI-005) — center must have UPI details configured before links can be generated
- Enables: UPI-002 (payment status auto-reconciliation), UPI-003 (payment history log)

**Definition of Done:**
- [ ] All AC pass in QA on minimum-spec Android (Redmi/Realme, 2GB RAM, Android 10+)
- [ ] WhatsApp deep link tested on both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business
- [ ] EC-01 through EC-04 tested and confirmed
- [ ] Code reviewed and merged

## Story UPI-002: Auto-reconcile UPI payment confirmation against fee record

**As a** Rahul (Center Director)
**I want** UPI payment confirmations to automatically update the child's outstanding balance in the platform
**So that** I don't have to manually mark payments as received or cross-check my UPI inbox against the billing records

**Inspired by:** PractiPal payment reconciliation; Practo UPI callback pattern

**Context:** Meena has completed payment via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM using the link Rahul shared. The platform needs to receive confirmation and update the record. This happens in the background — Rahul may not be actively using the app.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] AC-01: Given a UPI payment has been successfully completed by the parent, when the UPI payment gateway sends a confirmation callback, then the system marks the corresponding payment request as "Paid", credits the amount to the child's fee record, and reduces the outstanding balance within 60 seconds of confirmation
- [ ] AC-02: Given a payment is confirmed, when Rahul next opens the billing screen (or if the app is active), then a push notification is displayed: "Payment received: ₹[amount] from [Parent Name] for [Child Name]"
- [ ] AC-03: Given a payment is confirmed, the system auto-generates a receipt (per REC-001 in the Invoicing epic) and queues it for delivery to the parent
- [ ] AC-04: Given a UPI payment fails (insufficient funds, cancelled, expired), the system marks the payment request as "Failed" and surfaces this status on the child's billing record with a timestamp; Rahul can regenerate a new link from the same screen
- [ ] AC-05: Given a payment is partially completed (amount paid less than link amount — edge case in some UPI implementations), the system records the amount actually received, calculates the remaining balance, and flags the record as "Partially Paid" for Rahul's review

**Edge Cases & Error States:**
- [ ] EC-01: If the UPI callback is delayed beyond 5 minutes (network issue), the system should not show a false "pending" indefinitely — it must re-query payment status after 5 minutes and again after 30 minutes; if unconfirmed after 2 hours, mark as "Status Unknown" and alert Rahul to verify manually
- [ ] EC-02: If a duplicate callback is received (gateway retry), the system must be idempotent — the same transaction reference ID must not result in a double credit
- [ ] EC-03: If the callback is received for a payment request that has already expired, the system logs it without applying the credit and alerts Rahul for manual resolution

**Non-Functional Requirements:**
- Performance: Reconciliation applied within 60 seconds of gateway callback receipt
- Offline: Callback processing is server-side; client reflects updated status on next app open or background sync
- Privacy: ⚠️ DPDPA — UPI transaction reference IDs and payment amounts are financial data stored against the child record; access restricted to center admin role only; not visible to therapists

**Dependencies:**
- Blocked by: UPI-001 (payment link generation), UPI gateway integration (infrastructure dependency)
- Enables: UPI-003 (payment history log), INV-004 (receipt generation)

**Definition of Done:**
- [ ] All AC pass in QA; payment status updates confirmed end-to-end with sandbox UPI gateway
- [ ] Idempotency tested with duplicate callback simulation
- [ ] EC-01 re-query logic tested
- [ ] Code reviewed and merged

## Story INV-002: Auto-generate monthly invoice from attendance records

**As a** Rahul (Center Director)
**I want** the platform to auto-generate a draft invoice for each family at the end of the billing cycle based on confirmed session attendance
**So that** I don't have to manually count sessions in a paper register and calculate amounts in Excel every month

**Inspired by:** Jane App session-based billing; TherapEZ invoicing; SimplePractice invoice generation

**Context:** End of month (or configurable billing cycle). Rahul opens the billing section and sees a batch of draft invoices ready for review. He reviews, adjusts where needed, and sends.

## ⚠️ Feature Factory Disclaimer

These features were defined by competitive observation and category assumption —
not by validated user research. Before committing engineering capacity, a real
product thinker should ask:

**What we copied but haven't validated:**

- UPI-Native Billing: [ASSUMPTION] Rahul prefers a structured UPI payment link flow over his current method of sharing his personal UPI ID via WhatsApp — and will actually adopt the platform for billing rather than continuing to use WhatsApp and manual reconciliation. H-12 in the hypothesis register directly tests this. Risk: Medium.
- UPI-Native Billing: [ASSUMPTION] Meena will click a payment link in WhatsApp and complete a UPI payment rather than paying cash at the center or doing a manual NEFT. Indian payment behavior at small private health centers (vs. e-commerce) is unvalidated.
