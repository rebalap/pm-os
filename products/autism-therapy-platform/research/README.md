# Research — Autism Therapy Platform

This folder contains all research outputs for the Autism Therapy Platform product.

---

## Folder Structure

### primary/
Outputs from original fieldwork conducted directly with users and stakeholders. This includes:
- Contextual inquiry notes and observations
- Semi-structured interview transcripts and synthesis
- Usability test protocols, recordings, and findings
- Diary study inputs and analysis

All files in this folder represent first-hand evidence collected from real participants.

### secondary/
Desk research, competitive analysis, and literature reviews. This includes:
- Competitive and comparable product analysis
- Market and industry landscape reviews
- Academic or clinical literature summaries relevant to ABA and autism therapy practice in India
- Regulatory and compliance reference documents (RCI, DPDPA 2023, RPWD Act 2016)

Secondary research informs hypotheses and frames primary research; it does not substitute for direct user evidence.
