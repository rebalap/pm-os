# Screening Questionnaire: Research Participant Identification
**Product:** Autism Therapy Platform (India)
**Stage:** Discovery
**Purpose:** Screen and route candidates for 1-hour virtual semi-structured interviews
**Target audiences:** Center Directors / Admin Managers AND Special Educators / Behavior Therapists
**Estimated completion time:** 5–7 minutes
**Administered via:** Google Form link shared over WhatsApp / email / referral network

---

> **Researcher note:** This questionnaire has two tracks. Questions Q1–Q5 are universal and determine routing. Questions Q6–Q12 are track-specific. Do not merge tracks in a single form view — use conditional logic (Google Forms branching or equivalent) to show only the relevant track after Q4.
>
> **Disposition logic is at the end of each track.** Mark each respondent as QUALIFY / WAITLIST / DISQUALIFY before reaching out to schedule.

---

## Introduction (shown to all respondents)

> Hi — thank you for your interest in participating in our research.
>
> We are conducting confidential research to understand how autism therapy centers in India operate day to day. We want to speak with people who work inside these centers — both clinical staff and those who manage operations.
>
> This short questionnaire takes about 5 minutes. Based on your answers, we will reach out to schedule a 1-hour video call at a time that works for you. There is no product to evaluate — just an honest conversation about your work.
>
> All information shared is confidential and will not be shared with any third party. Your name will not appear in any research output.

---

> **Routing logic:** "Less than 6 months" → **DISQUALIFY** (cannot speak to established billing, dropout, and enrollment workflows). "6–12 months" → WAITLIST; recruit only if quota not met from more experienced candidates.

**Q8. Which of the following tools do you currently use to manage center operations?** *(Select all that apply)*

- [ ] WhatsApp (personal or WhatsApp Business)
- [ ] Paper registers or notebooks
- [ ] Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets
- [ ] A dedicated center management or practice management software *(open text: please name it)*
- [ ] Billing or accounting software (e.g., Tally, Vyapar, Zoho Books)
- [ ] None of the above

> **Routing logic:** If respondent selects "A dedicated center management or practice management software" **and** names a software that includes billing, scheduling, AND clinical records → **DISQUALIFY** (per recruitment criteria: do not recruit centers currently using dedicated management software — a separate segment). If they name Cognitivebotics alone, or name a billing-only tool, QUALIFY and flag for H-11 (Cognitivebotics gap hypothesis).
>
> "None of the above" → **QUALIFY** — likely an all-paper/verbal center; high research value.

### Overall Targets

| Segment | Target n | Priority |

### Pairing Flag
Where a qualified therapist names their center director, or a director offers to connect you with their therapist team — flag for paired recruitment. Paired interviews from the same center allow triangulation of H-05 (verbal program handover) from both sides.

### Cognitivebotics Flag
If any respondent (either track) mentions Cognitivebotics in Q8 or Q9 — flag immediately. These participants are critical for validating H-11 (whether Cognitivebotics covers trial-level data collection). Do not disqualify.
