# Therapist and Center Workflow Survey
**Product:** Autism Therapy Platform
**Research stage:** Discovery — generative
**Date:** April 2026
**Suggested platform:** Google Forms or Typeform

---

## Intro paragraph

This short survey is for special educators, behavior therapists, and therapy center directors working with children with autism in India. It takes about 8–10 minutes to complete. We are trying to understand how therapy centers currently manage their daily work — from sessions to paperwork to parent communication. There are no right or wrong answers. Your honest experience is what helps us most.
