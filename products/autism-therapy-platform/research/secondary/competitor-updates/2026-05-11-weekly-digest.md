- **CentralReach (CR Care Coordinator — parent training at scale):** CentralReach launched CR Care Coordinator, a patented parent training product enabling providers to deliver structured parent training via multimedia modules integrated into their EMR. Internal data across 3,600+ providers showed centers with highest parent training utilization saw ~30% reduction in learner churn. A webinar demo is scheduled for May 18, 2026. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/centralreach-announces-launch-cr-care-150000381.html) · [CentralReach blog](https://centralreach.com/blog/centralreach-announces-the-launch-of-cr-care-coordinator-its-patented-parent-training-software-product-that-enables-therapy-providers-to-deliver-parent-training-at-scale-empowering-families-and-ma/)) — *ATP implication: Parent training delivery is directly adjacent to ATP's patient enrollment and treatment plans workstreams. If Indian centers show the same churn-reduction pattern, a structured parent home-program feature becomes high-priority. WhatsApp-based parent updates are ATP's assumed gap — CentralReach is now solving this systematically in the US market.*

- **CentralReach + Theralytics + Raven Health (AI session notes now standard):** As of May 2026, 8 of 10 major US ABA platforms have launched AI session note generation — CentralReach (NoteDraftAI + NoteGuardAI), RethinkBH (Session Note AI), Theralytics, Raven Health, Ensora Health (AI Session Assistant), Artemis ABA, Passage Health (Frontera AI), and VGPM. Motivity and AlohaABA have not yet publicly launched AI note generation. ([VGSoft roundup, May 2026](https://vgsoft.co/blog/aba-session-notes-software-2026)) — *ATP implication: AI-assisted session documentation is becoming table stakes in the US ABA market within a 24-month window. This directly overlaps with ATP's in-session data collection and treatment plans workstreams. India adoption will lag, but the design bar is rising — therapists who visit US-style resources will expect this.*

- **Theralytics (live supervision mode + predictive analytics):** Theralytics offers real-time supervision mode — supervisors can watch live sessions, annotate programs mid-session, and push feedback to staff without interrupting the child. AI tools auto-generate session summaries and flag predicted skill plateaus. ([Theralytics blog](https://www.theralytics.net/blogs/best-aba-data-collection-software)) — *ATP implication: Real-time supervisor oversight is directly relevant to ATP's in-session data collection workstream. India centers with senior therapists supervising junior staff face the same coordination problem — this is a feature category ATP should explore in fieldwork.*

## 💡 Strategic Implication

US ABA platforms are converging on AI-assisted session documentation and structured parent training as the next competitive baseline — 8 of 10 platforms now have AI session notes, and CentralReach is productizing parent training with measurable churn impact. ATP's defensible position is not feature parity with these US tools but India-specificity: offline-first operation, RCI-aligned clinical workflows, sub-₹5,000/month price points, and Android-first design that works on mid-range devices — advantages no US platform is building toward.
