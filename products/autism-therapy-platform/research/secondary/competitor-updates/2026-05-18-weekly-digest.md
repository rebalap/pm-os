- **Gabify / Neurolens (India new entrant — patient enrollment):** Gabify, a New Delhi–based healthtech startup, raised $175,000 in pre-seed funding from Inflection Point Ventures and has expanded Neurolens, its AI-powered autism screening platform, to 100+ partner therapy centers across 50+ cities in India. Screenings start at Rs 499 and run as standardised assessments at Rs 999. Over 2,500 children have been screened to date. ([Economic Times](https://entrepreneur.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/funding/gabify-secures-175000-in-pre-seed-funding-to-transform-neurodevelopmental-disorder-detection-with-ai/129971856) · [BW Disrupt](https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/gabify-raises-175k-pre-seed-round-led-by-inflection-point-ventures-600556) · [The Better India](https://thebetterindia.com/startup/neurolens-by-gabify-ai-autism-screening-children-new-delhi-early-detection-india-10993636)) — *ATP implication: Gabify now has a presence inside 100+ Indian therapy centers as a screening and intake tool. ATP's patient enrollment workstream must determine whether centers see this as a complementary tool (feeding enrolled clients into ATP's session management) or a competing intake system. The Rs 499–999 price point also establishes a very low consumer willingness-to-pay anchor for screening — ATP's center-sold B2B model at ₹3,000–8,000/month per center sits in a different value layer, but awareness of this pricing context matters.*

- **DPDPA — Healthcare exemption confirmed, consent verification requirements clarified (treatment plans/billing):** The DPDP Rules 2025 explicitly confirm that healthcare providers may process children's personal data without standard parental consent where necessary for health purposes — but only under a strict "essential health purposes" standard. Data Fiduciaries must verify the identity of the person claiming to be a parent or guardian before processing a person with disability's data, and are prohibited from behavioral monitoring, tracking, and targeted advertising of children. Consent records must be maintained in immutable logs for 7 years. ([DLA Piper Data Protection](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=IN) · [Consent.in blog](https://www.consent.in/blog/child-consent) · [DPDPA.com Rules PDF](https://www.dpdpa.com/DPDP_Rules_2025_English_only.pdf)) — *ATP implication: The healthcare exemption reduces the consent friction for core clinical data collection (in-session logs, treatment plans, progress notes) but introduces a non-trivial guardian identity verification requirement. ATP's intake and enrollment flow must include a guardian verification step — design this into the patient enrollment workstream before moving to Define stage.*

## 🇮🇳 India Market Signals

- 🇮🇳 **Gabify expands to 100+ therapy centers across India:** Gabify Care now lists 2,500+ children screened, 100+ partner centres, 50+ cities covered, and 300+ clinicians on-platform. The platform connects families to "verified therapy centers" — positioning Gabify as both a screening tool and a therapy center marketplace. This creates a discovery and referral network that could affect how new patients find and enroll at centers. ([Gabify Care](https://gabify.care/))

- 🇮🇳 **DPDPA consent rules active — verifiable guardian identity required:** India's DPDP Rules 2025 place explicit obligations on any platform processing data of children or persons with disabilities. Self-declaration of parenthood is not sufficient — platforms must perform due diligence to verify guardian identity before processing. This applies directly to autism therapy software handling data of minor clients. ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=IN) · [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/ht-insight/governance/processing-children-s-personal-data-under-the-dpdpa-101778653754494.html))

- 🇮🇳 **India Autism Center 2026 tech roundup — AI diagnostics gaining traction:** India Autism Center's published 2026 blog notes growing adoption of four technology domains in India: AI-powered screening and early diagnosis, VR/AR-based therapy platforms, social robots, and personalized data-driven care. Cites 1-in-65–68 children on the autism spectrum in India. ([India Autism Center](https://indiaautismcenter.org/blog/latest-tech-in-autism/))

## 💡 Strategic Implication

Gabify's rapid expansion to 100+ Indian therapy centers as a screening and referral network is the most consequential India-specific signal this week. ATP's patient enrollment workstream should treat Gabify as a potential integration point rather than a direct competitor — if centers already use Gabify to screen incoming children, ATP could position its intake and enrollment module to receive Gabify-screened referrals rather than duplicate the screening function. Separately, the DPDPA guardian verification requirement is a concrete product design constraint that needs to be built into ATP's intake flow now, before the enrollment workstream moves from Discovery to Define.
