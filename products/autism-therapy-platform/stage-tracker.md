## Open Assumptions (Platform-Wide)

> [ASSUMPTION] = not yet validated. These drive research priorities.

- [ASSUMPTION] Special educators find paper/WhatsApp-based data collection disruptive enough to pay for a software alternative
- [ASSUMPTION] Cognitivebotics does not cover in-session behavioral data collection — gap exists for a complementary tool
- [ASSUMPTION] Clinical supervisors spend significant uncompensated time on manual progress report writing
- [ASSUMPTION] Offline-first is a hard requirement for most centers (vs. a nice-to-have)
- [ASSUMPTION] English-only UI is acceptable for therapist-facing features in metro markets at launch
- [ASSUMPTION] Center directors lack real-time visibility into utilization and compliance
- [ASSUMPTION] Parents want more transparency into session progress
- [ASSUMPTION] Patient intake and enrollment is managed manually (paper/Excel) and is a pain point for center directors
- [ASSUMPTION] Treatment plan generation is time-consuming and done manually by clinical supervisors
- [ASSUMPTION] Billing and follow-up reminders are not systematized — centers lose revenue due to missed follow-ups
- [ASSUMPTION] Center directors would pay for a unified admin tool if it reduced their patchwork of Excel/WhatsApp/paper
