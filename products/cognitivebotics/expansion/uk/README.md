## What This Initiative Is

Expansion of the Cognitivebotics platform into the United Kingdom market, serving neurodiverse children and their families through therapy centers, SEN schools, and NHS-linked pathways.

The UK market differs materially from India:
- NHS is the dominant healthcare system — private therapy exists but NHS referral pathways matter for scale
- SEN (Special Educational Needs) legislation and EHCP (Education, Health and Care Plan) processes shape how support is delivered in schools
- CQC (Care Quality Commission) and NICE (National Institute for Health and Care Excellence) are the relevant regulatory bodies
- Clinical workforce is predominantly NHS-trained; BCBA certification exists but is not the dominant credential
- Data protection: UK GDPR applies (post-Brexit equivalent of EU GDPR); different from India's DPDPA 2023

Each meeting folder contains:
- `agenda.md` — prepared before the meeting
- `notes.md` — raw notes captured during or immediately after
